# Role checklist for resume review (EXAMPLE: Technical Program Manager)

A **worked example**; replace for another role (see `docs/ADAPTING.md`). The recruiter lens and the six prompts stay the same; this file says what a screener for the target role looks for.

## What a TPM screener looks for in ten seconds

- A title and subhead that say program management in a named field (for example silicon, hardware, cloud infrastructure).
- Scale: number of teams, geographies, programs, schedule or budget size, each from the metrics worksheet.
- Outcomes: launches shipped on schedule, qualification cycle time, risk caught early, customer or partner results.
- Ownership verbs: owned, drove, led, delivered, not "supported" or "assisted".
- Tooling and methods named plainly (Jira, Confluence, risk and dependency management, executive reporting).

## Common weak spots

- Summary that lists adjectives instead of program types and scale.
- Bullets that describe meetings and status reports rather than results.
- A skills block with more than six items per row (wraps in a PDF) or skills with no bullet evidence.
- Customer-facing work described without saying which customer tier, if the claims limits restrict it.

## Role-specific traps in claims

Mirror the config's `do_not_claim` list: a certification not held, a customer tier not owned, an engineering skill not practiced.

## For another role

List the same four things for your role: what the screener scans for, the scale metrics that matter, the common weak spots, and your claim traps. For a data science engineer that would be model scope and business impact, datasets and scale, experiment rigor, production deployment, and claims such as "published" or "PhD" that must not be overstated.
