# Role vocabulary for tailoring (EXAMPLE: Technical Program Manager)

A **worked example**; replace this file for another role (see `docs/ADAPTING.md`). It supplies the wording guidance the tailoring loop applies. It never supplies facts: every fact comes from the master resume and metrics worksheet.

## Emphasis themes TPM postings usually carry

Program ownership end to end; cross-functional execution (engineering, test, manufacturing, customers); risk, dependency and escalation management; executive reporting; customer or partner programs; launch readiness; tooling (Jira, Confluence, scheduling and dashboard tools).

## Mirror, do not stuff

Mirror a posting's exact phrase only where the resume already evidences it ("new product introduction", "customer qualification", "bring-up"). If a phrase is missing from the resume and the work is real, ask the user to confirm and add it from their own words. If the work is not real, it is a gap: name it.

## Subhead and summary shapes (examples)

- "Senior Technical Program Manager | Silicon and hardware NPI | Customer qualification" (title, field, strongest verified hook)
- Summary opener: years of experience, the program types owned, the strongest verified scale or outcome from the metrics worksheet.

## Skills row

At most six items per row so the row does not wrap in a PDF; each item needs at least one bullet elsewhere that evidences it.

## Typical hard gaps (example; keep in sync with `do_not_claim`)

A certification the candidate does not hold; a customer tier or scope beyond what the metrics worksheet states; a design-engineering skill (RTL, verification) the candidate has not practiced.

## For another role

Write the same five parts for your role: the themes your target postings carry, the phrases you can truthfully mirror, subhead and summary shapes, the skills-row rule, and your typical hard gaps.
