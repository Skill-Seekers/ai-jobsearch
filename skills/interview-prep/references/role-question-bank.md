# Role question bank (EXAMPLE: Technical Program Manager)

A **worked example**; replace for another role (see `docs/ADAPTING.md`). Questions are prompts to practice with the candidate's own stories; nothing here is a fact about the candidate.

## Behavioral themes

Conflict across teams; a schedule that slipped; a decision with incomplete data; influencing without authority; a failure and what changed; managing a difficult stakeholder.

## Role-specific scenarios (type c)

- A critical dependency slips two weeks a month before launch. Walk through your first 24 hours.
- Engineering and a customer disagree on scope. How do you resolve it and what do you document?
- A go/no-go with unresolved quality issues. What evidence do you ask for and who decides?
- You inherit a program with no plan. What do you build first?
- Two programs compete for the same scarce team. How do you decide and communicate?

## Recruiter pre-screen prompts

Walk me through your background in 60 seconds; why this company; what are you looking for; compensation expectations; location and work mode; what would make you leave your current role.

## For another role

Write the same three lists for your role. For a data science engineer, scenarios would be: a model degrades in production, a stakeholder wants a metric you think is misleading, an experiment shows a flat result, you must choose between a simple and a complex model with a deadline, and a data quality issue is found after launch.
