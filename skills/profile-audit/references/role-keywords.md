# Role keywords for LinkedIn (EXAMPLE: Technical Program Manager)

A **worked example**; replace for another role (see `docs/ADAPTING.md`). Keywords are candidates to test, not claims: use one only where the resume evidences it.

## Title and headline terms recruiters search

Technical Program Manager, Senior / Staff / Principal TPM, Engineering Program Manager, Program Manager, NPI, New Product Introduction.

## Field terms (pick only the ones that are true)

Silicon, ASIC, SoC, bring-up, qualification, firmware, hardware, platform, data center, cloud infrastructure, storage, networking, AI infrastructure.

## Method and tool terms

Cross-functional leadership, risk management, dependency management, stakeholder management, executive reporting, roadmap, launch readiness, Agile, Jira, Confluence.

## Density guidance

Headline: two or three of the strongest terms. About: the field terms in natural sentences. Experience: each role names the program type and the tools once. Avoid repeating a term to game search.

## For another role

List the title terms recruiters search for your role, the field terms that are true for you, and the method and tool terms. Your config's `skill_vocabulary` is the best starting list.
